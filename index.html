    How Elo works

    Each day follows the same loop: warm up, learn one idea, record a 60–90 second take, listen back, score it, adjust, record again. Then use the idea in a real conversation the same day. Using it in real meetings is what makes it stick.

    Recording

    Elo uses your phone's microphone in two ways at once. It records audio so you can play the take back, and Apple's speech recognition writes a live transcript, which drives the word count and the filler count.

    Words per minute updates live while you speak. If the transcript isn't coming through, Elo estimates it from the rhythm of your voice, counting syllables, and marks it “est.”. Each take with a working transcript recalibrates the estimate to how you speak.

    Recording mode

    If the live transcript and the recording clash on your phone (for example, the transcript stops as soon as you start), choose one of the single modes.

    Transcript language

    What the scores mean

    Pace: conversational speech usually runs at about 140–160 words per minute, and the score drops the further you drift outside that band. Fillers: counted from the transcript plus your playback tally. Zero per minute scores 100, and each filler per minute costs 12 points. Pauses: silences of half a second to four seconds between bursts of speech. As fillers fall, this number should rise. Vocal confidence and structure: rate these yourself from the playback, or use “Copy for Claude” and paste the take into a chat for a full critique.

    Honest limits

    Apple's speech recognition usually strips out “um” and “uh”, so the live filler count will under-report them. The playback tally is the accurate count. Live transcription needs Siri & Dictation switched on in Settings. If it doesn't work from the home-screen icon, open Elo in Safari instead. The words-per-minute estimate is approximate until a few transcribed takes have calibrated it.

    Your filler list

    Comma-separated. Remove “like” if you use it correctly a lot and it's inflating your count.
